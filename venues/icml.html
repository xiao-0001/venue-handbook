頁數與格式

  • 主文最多 8 頁，之後可接任意頁數的參考文獻與附錄，全部放在單一 PDF。
    官方原文
    Submitted papers are composed of a main body, which can be up to eight pages long, followed by any number of pages for references and appendices, all in a single PDF file.

    出處：ICML 2026 作者說明（2026/11/02 擷取）

  • 主文超過 8 頁的投稿會被自動拒絕。
    官方原文
    In particular, any submission whose main body goes over the 8 page limit will be automatically rejected.

    出處：ICML 2026 作者說明（2026/11/02 擷取）

  • Impact statement 放在論文末（與 Acknowledgements 同處、References 前），不計入頁數上限。
    官方原文
    This statement should be in a separate section at the end of the paper (co-located with Acknowledgements, before References), and does not count toward the paper page limit.

    出處：ICML 2026 徵稿說明（2026/11/02 擷取）

  • camera-ready 多 1 頁，主文上限為 9 頁。
    官方原文
    In the camera-ready version, you have an extra page to address reviewer comments, so the length limit of the paper body is 9 pages

    出處：ICML 2026 作者說明（2026/11/02 擷取）

  • 審稿期間任何時候都不得把論文宣傳為 ICML 投稿。
    官方原文

    官方原文
    If the authors have posted or plan to post a non-anonymized version of the paper online before the ICML decisions are made, the submitted version must not refer to the non-anonymized version.

  • 投稿中不應包含致謝、計畫編號或公開程式碼庫連結。

附錄與補充材料

  • 審稿人可自行決定是否閱讀附錄或補充材料，關鍵內容必須放在主文。
    官方原文
    It is entirely up to the reviewers to decide whether they wish to consult any of the appendices in the submitted paper or this Supplementary Material. Therefore, if there is material critical to the evaluation of the paper, it needs to be included in the main body of the paper.

    出處：ICML 2026 作者說明（2026/11/02 擷取）

  • 傳統文字附錄不必另交補充材料，可直接放在主投稿檔。
    官方原文

  • 補充材料沒有另外的截止日，須與論文同時提交。
    官方原文
    There is no separate deadline for Supplementary Material: All supplementary material must be submitted by the same deadline as the paper submission.

    出處：ICML 2026 作者說明（2026/11/02 擷取）
